# PokéSurvivor Online

A free, single-player browser survival game featuring Pokémon partners,
automatic attacks, and five-minute forest expeditions.

**[Play PokéSurvivor Online](https://pokesurvivor.online/)**

No download or account required. Play with keyboard controls on desktop
or touch controls on mobile.

## Preview

![PokéSurvivor Online preview](./images/preview.png)

## Features

- Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Pikachu.
- Collect experience and upgrade automatic attacks.
- Combine up to four moves and evolve signature attacks.
- Survive a five-minute forest run and defeat two bosses.
- Spend earned coins on permanent camp upgrades.
- Discover enemies and complete your field journal.
- Keep completed-run progress in your browser.

## How to play

1. Open the game website and choose your partner.
2. Move with WASD or arrow keys, or drag inside the arena on mobile.
3. Collect experience crystals and choose upgrades as you level up.
4. Survive to five minutes and defeat both bosses.

Progress is stored locally in your browser and does not sync between devices.
Closing or reloading the tab ends the active run.
